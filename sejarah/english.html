History

History of the Study Program

The journey of Physics Education from its origins as Tadris Physics to achieving "Unggul" (Superior) accreditation

The Physics Education Study Program originated from the Natural Sciences (IPA) Study Program, Department of Tadris, Faculty of Tarbiyah at IAIN Sunan Kalijaga. Officially, the Physics Education Study Program was established in 1999 pursuant to Decree of the Director General of Islamic Institutional Development No. E/58/1999 dated March 29, 1999.

The Natural Sciences program at the Faculty of Tarbiyah was subsequently developed into three independent study programs: Physics Education, Chemistry Education, and Biology Education, based on Decree of the Director General of Islamic Institutional Development No. E/47/2001 dated March 20, 2001.

Following the transformation of IAIN into State Islamic University (UIN) in 2004, pursuant to Presidential Decree No. 50 of 2004 dated June 21, 2004, this study program grew rapidly. Structural reforms and curricular enhancements grounded in knowledge integration were carried out, and the faculty was renamed the Faculty of Tarbiyah and Education Sciences (FITK).

Physics Education has undergone multiple accreditation assessments — in 2008, 2014, and 2018 — with a rating of "B". Through continuous dedication to delivering quality education, relevant curriculum development, increased faculty research productivity, and community service, the study program achieved "Unggul" (Superior / Excellent) accreditation in 2023.

Today, Physics Education moves forward with a renewed spirit to nurture graduates who are professional, innovative, character-driven, and ready to compete at both national and global levels.

Curriculum

Curriculum History

Continuous curriculum development reviewed every 4 years involving lecturers, students, alumni, and stakeholders

2013

2013 Curriculum (KKNI Era)

Adjusted in response to Law No. 12/2012 on Higher Education and the implementation of the Indonesian National Qualifications Framework (KKNI).

  • Transition from content-based orientation to learning outcome competencies
  • Aligned with KKNI Level 6 (Bachelor's degree standard)
2017

KKNI & SNPT Curriculum

Strengthened and refined to reinforce the graduate profile of prospective professional physics educators.

  • Integrative-interconnective paradigm unique to UIN Sunan Kalijaga
  • Alignment with National Higher Education Standards (SNPT)
2019

Pre-MBKM Transitional Curriculum

Transitional foundation ahead of the national higher education policy disruption era.

  • Restructuring of course distribution and semester credit units (SKS)
  • Strengthened integration of Islamic scholarship and natural sciences
2020

MBKM Curriculum

Responding to the Merdeka Belajar Kampus Merdeka (MBKM) policy introduced by the Ministry of Education. On July 9, 2022, an intensive redesign of the MBKM curriculum was carried out.

  • Teaching practicum internships (School Field Practice / PLP)
  • Research internships (output: academic journal articles)
  • Village development internships (integrated with KKN)
2024

OBE (Outcome-Based Education) Curriculum

Comprehensive curriculum systematically oriented toward Learning Outcomes / Program Learning Outcomes (CPL / PLO).

  • Minor evaluations conducted every 4 semesters
  • Major evaluations conducted every 4 years
  • Informed by tracer study results (graduates & employers)
  • Benchmarked against national and international accreditation standards
